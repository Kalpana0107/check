How AutoGrade AI Works

A simple, 3-step process to reclaim your time and provide faster feedback to students.

1. Upload Papers

Scan or photograph student answer sheets and upload them as images.

2. Provide Key

Upload your answer key or grading rubric as a standard PDF document.

3. AI Evaluation

Our NLP engine extracts text, comprehends meaning, and assigns a score.
